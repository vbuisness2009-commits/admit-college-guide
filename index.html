About AdmitAI: Philosophy & Architecture

Our Mission

With national high school counselor ratios exceeding 400:1, students and families are often targeted by commercial platforms charging fees for speculative "chance calculators." AdmitAI provides a 100% transparent, free, and fact-based higher education intelligence platform based solely on codified statutes and verified federal records.

Core Engines

  • Deterministic Statutory Rule Engine: Evaluates multi-state auto-admission policies (Texas TEC § 51.803, California UC Regents 2102, Washington GAP, Florida BOG 6.002) directly against student rank and course benchmarks.
  • Federal Scorecard Percentile Engine: Visualizes official middle-50% SAT/ACT ranges from the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard with precise delta targets.
  • AP/IB Higher Education Credit Engine: Maps exams against verified university registrar catalogs and state credit laws, calculating course code waivers and net tuition dollars saved.
  • Counselor Advisory Dossier: Generates clean, audit-ready PDF summary reports for guidance counselors and family college planning meetings.

Official Data Sources & Methodology

1. Authoritative Public Data Sources

Every numerical distribution and policy cutoff in AdmitAI is backed by authoritative public-domain sources:

  • U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard: Official federal database for 25th/75th percentile SAT/ACT ranges, graduation rates, net price, and average tuition.
  • State Automatic Admission Statutes: California UC Regents Policy 2102, Washington RCW 28B.77.020, Georgia USG Policy 4.2, Florida BOG Reg 6.002, and Texas Education Code § 51.803.
  • State AP Credit Legislation: California AB 1985, Illinois Public Act 099-0358, Ohio ORC 3333.163, Florida Title XLVIII, and Texas Education Code § 51.968.
  • University Registrar Catalogs: Direct credit equivalency tables curated from university testing service and registrar offices.

2. The Anti-Hallucination Standard

Commercial admissions platforms use black-box formulas to output speculative "chance percentages". Because university admissions involve holistic evaluations, chance estimates are mathematically unverifiable. AdmitAI strictly outputs verifiable facts: published score distributions, exact course waivers, and codified statutory rules.

3. Data Freshness & Verification

AdmitAI tracks annual College Scorecard data releases and legislative statutory revisions, ensuring all score benchmarks, tuition credit evaluations, and statutory eligibility criteria reflect current institutional and legal standards.
