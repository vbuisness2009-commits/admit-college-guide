Congressional App Challenge: Technical Brief & Showcase

The Civic Problem Solved

The national public high school student-to-counselor ratio exceeds 400:1, leaving millions of students—particularly low-income and first-generation candidates—without individualized college guidance. Meanwhile, commercial platforms monetize anxiety using black-box "chance predictor" formulas that mislead students. AdmitAI democratizes admissions data by functioning as an open, 100% fact-based public utility that never predicts or guesses.

Core Technical Architecture

  • Deterministic Statutory Rule Engine: A multi-state logical evaluator (`StatusEngine`) that parses state education codes (Texas TEC § 51.803, California UC Regents 2102, Washington GAP, Florida BOG 6.002) against verified coursework and rank benchmarks without probabilistic guesswork.
  • Federal Scorecard Percentile Visualizer: Pulls official middle-50% SAT/ACT ranges from the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard, plotting student scores on a normalized geometric continuum with precise target point deltas.
  • Higher Education Credit & Tuition ROI Engine: Cross-references student AP/IB scores against institutional registrar matrices and state AP credit statutes (e.g. Texas HB 1992, California AB 1985), itemizing replaced college course codes and calculating net tuition savings.
  • Interactive What-If Goal Simulator: Real-time reactive state modeling allowing students to adjust test scores or GPA targets and immediately observe changes in university range classifications.
  • Counselor Advisory Dossier Generator: Generates a printable, audit-grade document with verified hashes for guidance counselor meetings and parental review.

Official Data Sources & AI Tool Disclosure

1. Official Public Data Sources

Every numerical distribution and policy cutoff in AdmitAI is backed by authoritative public-domain sources:

  • U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard: Official federal database for 25th/75th percentile SAT/ACT ranges, graduation rates, net price, and average tuition.
  • State Automatic Admission Statutes: California UC Regents Policy 2102, Washington RCW 28B.77.020, Georgia USG Policy 4.2, Florida BOG Reg 6.002, and Texas Education Code § 51.803.
  • State AP Credit Legislation: California AB 1985, Illinois Public Act 099-0358, Ohio ORC 3333.163, Florida Title XLVIII, and Texas Education Code § 51.968.
  • University Registrar Catalogs: Direct credit equivalency tables curated from university testing service offices.

2. The Anti-Hallucination Core Principle

Commercial admissions platforms use proprietary statistical formulas to output speculative "chance percentages". Because university admissions involve qualitative, holistic evaluations, chance estimates are mathematically unverifiable. AdmitAI strictly outputs verifiable facts: published score distributions, exact course waivers, and statutory rules.
